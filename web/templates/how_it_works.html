How post-kit Works

Your AI CLI agent creates content. This dashboard shows everything.

The Flow

1

You talk to your AI CLI

Open kiro, claude, cursor — any AI CLI tool. Say "write a post about [topic]". The AI reads your config files and runs the full pipeline.

2

Pipeline runs automatically

Research → Write → Voice Match → Anti-AI Pass → Quality Gate. Your voice, your rules, your style — enforced every time.

3

Content delivered with everything

You get: the post (per platform), hashtags, image prompt, video prompt, references — all generated together.

4

Saved to database + filesystem

Every post is stored in SQLite with full metadata. View, copy, manage everything from this dashboard.

What Each Post Contains

📝 Post content

Platform-specific version (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Reddit)

#️⃣ Hashtags

Platform-aware (LinkedIn yes, X never, Insta in comments)

🖼️ Image prompt

For Midjourney/DALL-E — copyable

🎬 Video prompt

30-sec Veo3/Runway script with voiceover

📎 References

Sources with URLs — always research-backed

🏷️ Metadata

Platform, type, CLI used, date, save path

What to Say to Your AI CLI

"write a post about [topic]"
"today's post" — researches trending news and creates for all platforms
"meme about [thing]" — uses meme templates
"roast [company/thing]"
"what's trending" — finds topics from your sources
"help me improve my voice" — brainstorm session

Built-in Quality Standards

✓ Every post is research-backed with real sources
✓ Anti-AI writing pass removes robotic language
✓ Quality gates: hook test, one-idea test, save/share test
✓ Platform-specific formatting (no hashtags where they hurt)
✓ Engagement hooks on every post
✓ Company tagging per your preference
✓ Accuracy over virality — funny AND true, always
